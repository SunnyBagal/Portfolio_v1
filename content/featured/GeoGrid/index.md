A geospatial lead-generation platform that helps sales teams discover and qualify local businesses on a map. Users define an area, and the app pulls nearby businesses, enriches each one with location and contact data, and organizes the results into exportable lead lists. Heavy scraping and enrichment run asynchronously through a BullMQ queue backed by Redis, so the UI stays responsive while jobs process in the background. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, and BullMQ during my internship at InteleCorp, where I led the technical build as Technical Head.
